New `@openrampkit/adapter-bridge`: Bridge virtual bank accounts for deposits (ACH, wire, SEPA, SPEI, Pix, Faster Payments into USDC on Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche and Solana), with the Bridge KYC link flow, and payouts from USDC to a US bank account or IBAN through Bridge transfers. Webhooks are verified with the endpoint's RSA public key.
