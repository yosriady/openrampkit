New `@openrampkit/adapter-kotani`: Kotani Pay (API v3) for African payment rails. Deposits: M-Pesa and mobile money (KE, GH, UG, TZ, ZM, RW, CM, CI, SN, CD) and bank checkout (ZA; NG is TO VERIFY) to USDC and USDT on Base, Polygon, Ethereum and Solana. Withdrawals: USDC or USDT to a mobile money wallet. It has a live country catalog, signed webhooks (`X-Kotani-Signature`) and optional request signing. Kotani Pay has a self-serve sandbox.
