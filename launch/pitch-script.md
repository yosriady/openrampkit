# Pitch video script (you on camera, 90 to 110 seconds)

Read it in your own words. Look at the camera. One take is fine.

## 1. Who you are (15 s)

Hi, I'm Yos. I'm based in Singapore. I've spent my career on payments and onchain infrastructure: engineering at PayPal and Grab, then Chainlink Labs. Today I also run Formo, analytics for onchain apps.

## 2. The problem (20 s)

At Formo, I see the same drop-off again and again: the first deposit. Most people in the world don't pay with a card. They pay from a bank app: Pix, UPI, SEPA, or a QR code. But crypto onramps are built for cards. So every team rebuilds the same integrations, and still loses users at the first step.

## 3. What I'm building (30 s)

OpenRampKit is the RainbowKit for onramps and deposits. It's open source. A developer adds one component and runs one small server. Users pay the way they already pay, and stablecoins land on Solana, Arbitrum, Base or Tempo. A planner compares routes across ten providers and picks the best one. The same kit handles withdrawals. There is an onchain settlement contract that can deposit straight into a vault, and AI agents can ask a person to top up with a pay link.

## 4. Why me (15 s)

I've built payments at scale, I've built onchain building blocks, and I've built developer tools people use. I grew up in Southeast Asia, where everyone pays by QR. This is the problem I understand best.

## 5. Where it's going (15 s)

It's MIT licensed and live today, with a demo you can try. Next: live provider partners, design partner pilots, and a hosted version for teams that don't want to run a server. If you build an app that needs deposits, I'd love to talk.
